Lifecycle Entry & Contact Routing Architecture

Decision-tree logic determining how new signups, invited collaborators, and inbound demo requests enter and transition across onboarding tracks based on intent, enrichment, and billing state.

Solid arrows indicate initial enrollment paths. Dashed arrows indicate behavioral state transitions (e.g., a solo user inviting a teammate mid-trial).

System Design Highlight: Separating initial signup intent from real-time behavioral triggers prevents single-user messaging from firing after a workspace becomes collaborative, ensuring seat expansion nudges align with actual workspace state.
