Next Best Action (NBA) Segmentation & Revenue Map

How historical cohort telemetry, firmographics, and campaign engagement signals are synthesized into revenue-tied lifecycle plays.

Methodology & Revenue Attribution Thesis (Sanitized Portfolio Sample) • Built by analyzing historical customer expansion and churn cohorts to identify which combination of account capacity (size/revenue), current product adoption, and channel responsiveness predicts conversion.
• Every lifecycle branch maps to a single commercial KPI (Net New ARR, Seat Expansion ARR, Plan Upgrade MRR, or Gross Retention) rather than vanity open/click metrics.
Stage 1 • Multi-Dimensional Customer Scoring Inputs
🏢 Firmographics & Capacity CRM / Enriched
  • Company Size: Solo (1), SMB (2–49), Mid-Market (50–249), Enterprise (250+).
  • Annual Revenue & ARR Potential: Total addressable seat ceiling vs. current ACV.
  • Department / Role: Individual contributor vs. functional team lead or ops buyer.
📊 Product Usage Telemetry Warehouse / BI
  • Current Feature Mix: Core free utility vs. collaborative/shared workflows vs. governance tools.
  • Usage Velocity & Depth: Weekly active days, asset volume, and paywall limit hits.
  • Cohort Benchmarks: Comparing current usage against historical converters at Day 14/30/60.
📨 Campaign & Channel Signals MAP / Engagement
  • Email & Nurture Response: Click intent on feature vs. pricing/ROI themes; fatigue thresholds.
  • Event & Content Intent: Webinar attendance, demo video completion, or help-doc visits.
  • Channel Suppression: Auto-pausing low-response email tracks in favor of in-app or AE outreach.
Decision Engine: Cohort propensity model evaluates all 3 dimensions daily to assign 1 mutually exclusive Next Best Action.
