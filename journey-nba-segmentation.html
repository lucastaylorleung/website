Next Best Action (NBA) Segmentation & Revenue Map

How enriched account growth signals (Clay & ZoomInfo), product telemetry, and campaign engagement are synthesized into CQL and revenue-tied lifecycle plays.

Methodology & Revenue Attribution Thesis (Sanitized Portfolio Sample) • Built by analyzing historical customer expansion and churn cohorts to identify which combination of Clay & ZoomInfo account growth enrichment (headcount velocity, firmographic size, revenue), current product adoption, and channel responsiveness predicts conversion.
• Every post-purchase and trial lifecycle branch maps directly to a commercial KPI (Customer Qualified Leads [CQLs], Cross-Sell/Expansion ARR, Plan Upgrade MRR, or Gross Retention) rather than vanity open/click metrics.
Stage 1 • Multi-Dimensional Customer Scoring & Enrichment Inputs
🏢 Clay & ZoomInfo Enrichment Account Growth
  • Growth & Hiring Velocity: Clay & ZoomInfo waterfall enrichment tracking 6-month headcount growth, new office openings, and department expansion.
  • Company Size & Revenue Tier: Solo (1), Small Business (2–49), Mid-Market (50–249), Enterprise (250+) and estimated annual revenue ceiling.
  • Technographics & Whitespace: Adjacent tools in stack indicating cross-sell readiness for add-on modules.
📊 Product Usage Telemetry Warehouse / BI
  • Current Feature & Product Mix: Core base plan usage vs. collaborative workflows, add-on modules, and governance features.
  • Usage Velocity & Depth: Weekly active days, asset volume, seat saturation, and paywall limit hits.
  • Cohort Benchmarks: Comparing live account telemetry against historical expanders at Day 30/60/90+.
📨 Campaign & Channel Signals MAP / Engagement
  • Email & Nurture Response: Click intent on feature vs. pricing/ROI themes; fatigue thresholds.
  • Event & Content Intent: Webinar attendance, product demo completion, or help-center visits.
  • Channel Suppression: Auto-pausing low-response email tracks in favor of in-product prompts or AE/CSM outreach.
AI-Assisted Decision Engine: Daily enrichment & propensity scoring assigns 1 mutually exclusive Next Best Action per account.
