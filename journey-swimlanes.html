TextExpander Onboarding Journey, by Persona

Day 1 to Day 60 today, built to extend into the post-60-day lifecycle. Source: Master Onboarding Email List (HubSpot).

Notes "Org Onboarding" and "Admin Onboarding" are two live, near-duplicate HubSpot sequences, merged into one Org / Team Admin lane below. A short 2-email "Individual Onboarding owned by Ted" variant (Day 3 to 4) also runs for some individual signups. All emails shown below are live. Each lane ends with a Post-60 Lifecycle placeholder, ready for that sequence as it gets built.
Click any card to open that email's copy in the Onboarding Emails for Reverse Trial doc. Cards marked "Open sequence copy" have no separate section there and open the sequence tab.
Entry rules come from HubSpot segments, the copy doc, and #rev-ops threads. They have not been validated against the live workflows.
Demo requests under 10 seats are not on this board. They go to the Under 10 Demo Sequence, sent from Lucas's sequence.
🧑‍💻 Individual Solo signup Works alone, no team features.
How they enter: Chose "Just me" at signup, or is an org of one that never adds members. A private domain with 0 to 9 employees (per enrichment) gets the Individual TED version, where Day 4 invites a team.
