B2B SaaS Onboarding Journey, by Persona

Day 1 to Day 60 reverse-trial lifecycle architecture, structured to scale into post-60-day retention & expansion. (Scroll horizontally per lane →)

Architecture Notes (Sanitized Portfolio Sample) • Consolidates overlapping legacy onboarding workflows into mutually exclusive persona tracks based on signup intent, workspace role, and billing tier.
• Incorporates a 30-day reverse-trial model: users receive full premium access for 30 days before transitioning to a perpetual free tier with targeted Day 45 and Day 60 conversion touchpoints.
• High-fit enterprise accounts branch dynamically at Day 60 based on seat count and firmographic enrichment, while inbound demo requests under 10 seats route to a dedicated sales-assist cadence.
💻 Individual User Solo signup Single-seat workspace without team collaboration features.
Entry Criteria: Selected "Just me" at signup, or remains a single-user workspace throughout the 30-day trial. Enriched domains with 1–9 employees receive a team-invitation variant on Day 4.
